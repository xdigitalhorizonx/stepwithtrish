High desert

Flooring for Reno's high-desert climate: dry air, sun and wood movement

Reno's air is dry most of the year, the sun is strong at elevation and the daily temperature swing is wide. Any floor can work here if it is chosen for the room and acclimated properly; the wrong one, installed in a hurry, gaps, cups or fades.

Hardwood vs LVP

Hardwood vs luxury vinyl plank for Northern Nevada homes

Hardwood is the floor you refinish; luxury vinyl plank is the floor you replace. In a Reno or Sparks house the choice usually comes down to which rooms get wet, how much sun they take, and how long you plan to stay.

Mudrooms

Mudroom and entry flooring for Reno–Tahoe snow, salt and mud

Rigid-core luxury vinyl plank is the mudroom floor to start with in a Reno–Tahoe house: warm underfoot, waterproof at the surface, and able to run straight through into the kitchen. Porcelain tile is the other honest answer, and this note says when. Both shrug off snowmelt, road salt and grit; the difference is how they feel and how they meet the floors beyond the door.
