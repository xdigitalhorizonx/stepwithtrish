The studio

A floor is the one surface you touch every day. We treat it like it matters.

Most flooring is sold by the square foot. We choose it by the room — how the light lands at four o'clock, where the dog sleeps, which way the boards should run to make a hallway feel longer. Then we install it exactly as drawn.

The step

Three steps. No guesswork between them.

Every project climbs the same three treads, in the same order, with the same person walking you up them.

  1. Step 01

    Consult

    We start with your rooms — the light, the traffic, the pets, the plans — and come back with samples that fit them, not a catalogue.

  2. Step 02

    Design

    Tone, plank width, pattern, transitions and thresholds, drawn into one plan for the whole floor — so every doorway makes sense.

  3. Step 03

    Install

    Measured twice, scheduled once. Subfloors prepped, seams tight, edges clean — finished the way it was drawn.

In the light

Herringbone, honey oak, four o'clock sun.

Direction changes how a room reads. Herringbone pulls the eye down the length of a kitchen and makes a narrow plan feel wider — the kind of decision that's easy to get right on paper and expensive to get wrong on site.
